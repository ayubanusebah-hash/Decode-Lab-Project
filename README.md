Project 4:Visualization 

1. Sum of TotalPrice by Product — Bar Chart
This shows the total revenue generated by each product.
It adds up the TotalPrice for all orders belonging to each product.
The longer the bar, the more revenue that product generated.
From your dashboard, Chair generated the highest total sales value, while Phone was among the lower ones.
What it tells us: Which products contribute most to overall revenue.

2. Sum of Quantity by Product — Bar Chart
This shows the total number of units sold for each product.
It sums the Quantity for each product.
It helps compare product sales volume.
What it tells us: Which products are sold in the largest quantities.
